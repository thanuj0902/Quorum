Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Data We Collect

Quorum is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Verification queries: Claims you submit are sent to the Anthropic API for processing. We do not store your queries on our servers.
  • API keys: If you provide an Anthropic API key, it is used only for the current session and is never stored persistently on our servers.
  • Local history: Your verification history is stored entirely in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Quorum uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic API: For AI-powered fact verification. Your claims are processed by Anthropic's Claude model. Refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel: For hosting and serving the application.
  • Render: For backend API hosting.

Data Storage

All verification reports and history are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data is not transmitted to any server. If you clear your browser data, your history will be lost.

Cookies

Quorum does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

Children's Privacy

Quorum is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us via GitHub Issues.
